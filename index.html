b24-gcal-sync

A personal, single-user tool that copies the owner's work meetings from a corporate Bitrix24 calendar into a dedicated calendar in the owner's own Google Calendar.

  • One-way synchronisation: Bitrix24 → Google Calendar.
  • Writes only to a calendar the tool creates itself (scope calendar.app.created); it cannot read or modify any other calendar of the account.
  • Runs only on the owner's own computer. There is no server, no other users, no third parties.

Privacy policy

Личный инструмент: переносит рабочие встречи владельца из Bitrix24 в его же Google Календарь.
